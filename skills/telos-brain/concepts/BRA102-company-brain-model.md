# Company Brain Model

A company brain is not a database. A database stores and waits to be searched. A brain answers, feeds every tool you use, and stays alive. The difference is not storage — it is that knowledge starts working without being asked.

| | Database (wiki / SharePoint / Notion) | Company Brain |
|---|---|---|
| **Access** | Stores, then waits to be searched | Answers — with the source, not 14 docs to wade through |
| **Reach** | Only helps if a human goes looking | Feeds every tool — AI starts already knowing the business |
| **Freshness** | Goes stale the moment work moves on | Stays alive — flags contradictions and stale info as work happens |

---

## The DNA genome

A brain's identity is load-bearing. Get the DNA wrong and you learn the wrong company faster. A brain is defined by six components:

| Component | What it captures |
|---|---|
| **Purpose** | Why the business exists |
| **Edge** | How it wins |
| **Model** | How it makes money |
| **Voice** | How it sounds |
| **Doctrine** | The way of doing things, plus the non-negotiables |
| **Customers** | Who it serves |

The DNA is established first and informs everything else the brain learns.

---

## The three learning loops

Different knowledge moves at different speeds and requires different machinery. A healthy brain runs on three clocks:

| Loop | Cadence | How | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Memory** | Daily | Automated | Every job remembered — nothing starts from zero |
| **Craft** | Weekly | Curated | What worked becomes reusable process (Skill Books) |
| **Compass** | Monthly | In-person | Strategy keeps the brain pointed in the right direction |

### The connective tissue

Loops in isolation are just three separate logs. The value is the flow between them:

- **Promote (up):** Daily memory surfaces repeated patterns into weekly Craft; Craft flags tensions up to the Compass.
- **Align (down):** The Compass pushes back down to retire processes and memories that no longer fit.

Knowledge graduates up. Alignment flows down. That is the difference between a brain that compounds and a wiki that drifts.

---

## Why the Craft loop is critical

The Memory loop survives because it is automated. The Compass loop survives because it is a meeting. The Craft loop is the orphan — manual, unglamorous, the first thing a busy team skips — and it is the exact layer that stops the brain rotting. A brain without an active Craft loop will drift within weeks.
